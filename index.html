Built with care

Designed for responsible professional communication

Mental health communication carries real responsibility. The system is built with that in mind at every level.

Human review required

Every piece of content is reviewed and approved by you before it is published. The system never posts autonomously.

Professional tone safeguards

Structured communication guidelines are embedded into the system — preventing language that falls outside your professional standards.

Full auditability

Every post has a complete workflow history — who approved it, when, and what was edited. A clear record you can refer back to.

Ethical communication support

Guidelines for responsible mental health messaging — avoiding stigmatising language and framing content with appropriate care.

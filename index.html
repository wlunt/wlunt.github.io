The workflow

From idea to published post — safely and automatically

Every piece of content passes through a structured process before it reaches your audience. Nothing is posted without your explicit approval.

Prompt → Engineering → Content → Review → Posting

You stay in control at every step. Nothing is published without approval.

Step by step

How the workflow operates

A structured process built on each step that takes your idea from prompt to published post — always with your oversight.

1
Step one

Prompt

What you do: Choose a topic relevant to your clients. A brief direction from you is all that's needed.

Managing anxiety before exams Understanding burnout Sleep hygiene tips Setting healthy boundaries

Your idea is transformed into a structured instruction the system can act on

2
Step two

Engineering

Behind the scenes: A structured AI agent system built in Python applies your practice's specific rules and guidelines.

  • Tone guidelines calibrated to your voice
  • Clinical communication rules and constraints
  • Platform-specific post structure and formatting
  • Consistency checks against your brand
  • Safety guardrails appropriate for mental health content

Not generic AI — a system engineered specifically for professional clinical use

3
Step three

Content generation

What it produces: Fully drafted content ready for your review.

  • Post body text — clear, professional, on-brand
  • Captions and variations for different platforms
  • Relevant hashtags
  • Platform-specific formatting adjustments

Content created quickly, consistently, and to a professional standard

4
Step four

Review and approval

Your control point: Every piece of content passes through you before anything happens. You read it, judge it, and decide what happens next.

  • Edit the draft directly
  • Approve it as written
  • Reject it and request a revision
  • Schedule it for a specific date and time

No content is ever posted without your explicit approval

5
Step five

Posting

Once you approve: The system handles publication across your chosen platforms, on schedule, without any further action from you.

  • Instagram
  • LinkedIn
  • Facebook & professional pages

Consistent posting without the administrative workload

The implementation process

How your system is designed and built

A structured collaborative process. We begin by understanding your practice, and we don't step back until the system genuinely reflects how you communicate.

1
Discovery

Understand your practice

We begin by listening. A conversation to understand how your practice operates and what it needs to communicate.

  • Your services and clinical specialties
  • Who your typical clients are
  • Your communication goals
  • Your professional boundaries
  • Your existing brand and online presence

A clear, shared understanding of how your practice communicates

2
Voice & brand

Define your voice and brand

We work with you to document exactly how your practice sounds — so the system can reproduce it reliably.

  • Tone of voice and register
  • Language preferences and vocabulary
  • Level of clinical detail
  • Topics to include and topics to avoid

Content that sounds consistent, professional, and recognisably yours

3
Configuration

Configure your agent

We build and configure the system based on everything we've learned. No guesswork — every setting reflects a decision made with you.

  • Content generation workflow
  • Platform selection and formatting rules
  • Posting schedule and frequency
  • Review and approval process
  • Brand rules and safety constraints

A customised system ready for controlled testing

4
Testing

Test and refine

We generate real drafts and review them together. If anything is off, we adjust the rules — not just the content.

  • Generate a range of draft posts
  • Review tone, clarity, and accuracy together
  • Adjust system rules where needed
  • Confirm the system performs reliably across different topics

Confidence that the system meets your professional standards before going live

5
Ongoing

Deploy and support

After launch, we stay involved. The system evolves as your practice does, and we're available to adjust it when your needs change.

  • Monitoring content performance and consistency
  • Adjusting tone and topic guidelines over time
  • Updating workflows if your practice changes

Reliable communication that evolves with your practice over time

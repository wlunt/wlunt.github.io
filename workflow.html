How the system works

Submit a single topic and the system handles the rest — from idea to published post with your approval at every stage.

(01)
Step one

Prompt

What you do: Choose a topic relevant to your clients. A brief direction from you is all that’s needed.

Managing anxiety before exams Understanding burnout Sleep hygiene tips Setting healthy boundaries

Your idea is transformed into a structured instruction the system can act on

→
(02)
Step two

Engineering

What we do: Our system applies your specific rules and guidelines:

  • Tone guidelines calibrated to your voice
  • Clinical communication rules and constraints
  • Platform-specific post structure and formatting
  • Consistency checks against your brand
  • Safety guardrails appropriate for mental health content

Our system creates a plan for turning your idea into content

→
(03)
Step three

Content generation

What we do: The system creates fully drafted content ready for your review.

  • Post body text — clear, professional, on-brand
  • Captions and variations for different platforms
  • Relevant hashtags
  • Platform-specific formatting adjustments

Content is created quickly, consistently, and to a professional standard

→
(04)
Step four

Review and approval

What you do: Read, judge, and decide what happens next. You can:

  • Edit the draft directly
  • Approve it as written
  • Reject it and request a revision
  • Schedule it for a specific date and time

No content is ever posted without your explicit approval

The implementation process

How your system is designed and built

A structured collaborative process. We begin by understanding your practice, and we don't step back until the system genuinely reflects how you communicate.

1
Discovery

Understand your practice

We begin by listening. A conversation to understand how your practice operates and what it needs to communicate.

  • Your services and clinical specialties
  • Who your typical clients are
  • Your communication goals
  • Your professional boundaries
  • Your existing brand and online presence

A clear, shared understanding of how your practice communicates

2
Voice & brand

Define your voice and brand

We work with you to document exactly how your practice sounds — so the system can reproduce it reliably.

  • Tone of voice and register
  • Language preferences and vocabulary
  • Level of clinical detail
  • Topics to include and topics to avoid

Content that sounds consistent, professional, and recognisably yours

3
Configuration

Configure your agent

We build and configure the system based on everything we've learned. No guesswork — every setting reflects a decision made with you.

  • Content generation workflow
  • Platform selection and formatting rules
  • Posting schedule and frequency
  • Review and approval process
  • Brand rules and safety constraints

A customised system ready for controlled testing

4
Testing

Test and refine

We generate real drafts and review them together. If anything is off, we adjust the rules — not just the content.

  • Generate a range of draft posts
  • Review tone, clarity, and accuracy together
  • Adjust system rules where needed
  • Confirm the system performs reliably across different topics

Confidence that the system meets your professional standards before going live

5
Ongoing

Deploy and support

After launch, we stay involved. The system evolves as your practice does, and we're available to adjust it when your needs change.

  • Monitoring content performance and consistency
  • Adjusting tone and topic guidelines over time
  • Updating workflows if your practice changes

Reliable communication that evolves with your practice over time
